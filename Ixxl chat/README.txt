Ixxl Chat - Room Context Menu Update

Replace your current liquid_glass_discord_chat.html with the included file.

Room actions:
- Right-click a room to open its custom menu (the browser context menu is suppressed).
- Double-click a room to open the custom menu.
- Click the visible three-dot button beside a room to open the same menu.
- Owners see room settings, pin/unpin, @everyone ping toggle, room-wide ban (where allowed), and delete (where allowed).
- Non-owners see only Copy room ID.
- Escape or clicking outside closes the menu.

No database migration is required for this UI-only change.
